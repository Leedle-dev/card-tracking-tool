Research Questions

1. Raw market trend
   - What is the current raw price?
   - What was the raw price at release?
   - What is the 30-day, 90-day, and 1-year trend where data exists?
   - How many recent sales are available?

2. Graded market
   - What active BGS listings exist?
   - What sold BGS comps exist?
   - What active PSA listings exist?
   - What sold PSA comps exist?
   - Are high listings credible or just aspirational?

3. Population and gem rate
   - What is the BGS population by grade?
   - What is the BGS Black Label population?
   - What is the PSA population by grade?
   - Does GemRate have grade-rate data for this exact card?
   - If Simplified Chinese data is sparse, is Japanese data a reasonable prior?

4. Print and condition assumptions
   - Is the English print likely to have lower gem rates than Japanese or Simplified Chinese?
   - Are there known condition issues for this card or set?
   - Does the local copy have centering, surface, corner, or edge risks?
